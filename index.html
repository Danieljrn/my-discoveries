Daniel Kambole, professionally known as Daniel JRN, is a Zambian entrepreneur, writer, and thinker, and the Founder of Keysounds Entertainment Records LTD. His work brings together creative expression, reflection, and a vision to create meaningful opportunities for emerging artists.

Born on March 31, 2002, in Matero, Lusaka, Zambia, Daniel started out as a musical artist and songwriter in 2019. Music became his first platform for expressing his thoughts, emotions, and experiences, laying the foundation for his broader creative and entrepreneurial journey.

In 2020, he recorded his first studio track, “Missing You,” marking an important milestone in his development as an independent artist. He later released his debut EP, #EmotionalStress, featuring “Emotional Thunder,” “From The First Sight,” “Over You,” “Every day,” and “Game Over.” His early musical work explored raw emotion and personal storytelling, drawing inspiration from artists such as XXXTentacion, Kendrick Lamar, J. Cole, and Zambia’s Zone Fam.

Over time, Daniel’s interests expanded beyond making music. Education, life experiences, and personal reflection deepened his interest in writing, exploring ideas, and building a business with purpose. While music remains part of his story, his present focus centers on entrepreneurship, writing, and thoughtful exploration of life and human experience.

Through his writing, Daniel shares discoveries and reflections on subjects such as kindness, love, thought, desire, persistence, and personal growth. His work expresses a desire to understand life more deeply and share ideas that encourage others to reflect on their own experiences and possibilities.

As Founder & Distribution Manager of Keysounds Entertainment Records LTD, Daniel is developing a platform that supports artistic growth, creativity, and professional development. His vision is to help emerging artists access meaningful opportunities and contribute to the growth of Zambia’s music scene.

From his beginnings as an artist to his evolving work as an entrepreneur, writer, and thinker, Daniel’s journey reflects a continuing commitment to expression, learning, and creating something meaningful.

The Power of Kindness, Generosity, and Love

By Daniel JRN   /   29 August 2026

Sometimes, the smallest act of kindness can become one of the biggest moments in someone's life—and the crazy part is, we mostly never know about it.

This is what I discovered about the power of being kind, generous, and loving in the most expressible way.

When you choose to be kind, generous, and loving, look for an opportunity to make a meaningful difference in someone's life—especially by doing something for them that nobody has done before. The moment you see an opportunity to help, take it as a chance to become part of their story in a way they may remember forever.

The truth is, it is possible to make your way into someone's life through a sincere act of kindness. Kindness is a superpower—and those who understand its power use it wisely.

Always remember that whenever you help someone, you are also helping yourself in the process. The good you give to others can create connections, opportunities, and memories that may return to you in ways you never expected.

And always remember that kindness, generosity, and love are among the few things that can leave a mark in someone's life forever— in a positive way.

Sometimes, the greatest kindness is simply choosing to help without waiting to be asked, when you genuinely believe your help could make a positive difference. It is one of the greatest forms of kindness because it comes from noticing someone's need and choosing to care enough to act.

The more you help others succeed, in anything, the more help you'll get as well. In the end, true richness is not measured only by what you have, but also by how many lives you have helped make better, because the richest person may not be the one who has the most, but the one who has given the most of themselves to help others rise.

The Greatest Forces and Powers

By Daniel JRN   /   23 August 2026

1. THE GREATEST FORCE EVER

The greatest asset humans have is THOUGHT—and, perhaps even more importantly, the ability to CONTROL IT.

The desire to express the thoughts within us may be one of the greatest forces humans will ever know.

I believe humans were created for expression—not to constantly hide, suppress, or fear what is within us, but to express ourselves in as many meaningful ways as possible.

The desire to express our thoughts through everything we do is a form of energy. I believe this energy needs to be released and directed every day.

That same energy can be redirected, through effort—whether small or great, depending on what we want to accomplish—toward creative work, quality time with friends or family, learning, building something, exercising, or simply engaging in an activity that brings us joy.

Without meaningful expression or activity, life can easily become boring, stagnant, or unfulfilling. We may go through our days without experiencing the sense of purpose, excitement, or satisfaction that makes life feel truly alive.

And beyond simply using this energy to enjoy life, I believe it is wise to redirect a significant portion of it toward work that can build something valuable—perhaps even something that can eventually create a fortune.

This energy exists within every person. As a person grows, the desire to express what is within them can grow as well.

I see this energy in the people who accomplish extraordinary things.

It is the same kind of inner drive that pushed Christ to preach the Gospel with conviction. It is the drive that pushed Michael Jordan to pursue basketball at an extraordinary level. It is the drive that pushed Muhammad Ali to become one of the greatest boxers of all time. It is the drive that pushed Cristiano Ronaldo toward becoming one of the greatest footballers of his generation. It is the drive behind the strength associated with Samson. And it is the same kind of inner force that gets countless people out of bed every morning to work, build, create, provide, and pursue something better.

I believe that when this energy is consistently neglected or has nowhere meaningful to go, a person can experience frustration, restlessness, emptiness, or unhappiness. And when it isn't consciously directed, it may naturally seek other forms of expression—sometimes through distractions or impulses that do not necessarily lead us toward the life we want.

That is why I believe we should make a deliberate effort to use this energy every day.

Redirect it.
Put it into something.
Create something.
Build something.
Learn something.
Love someone.
Work toward something.

And, if possible, direct some of it toward work that can create lasting value.

Redirecting this energy toward what we want to achieve may be one of the greatest keys to success.

This energy itself is neutral. It can be redirected into almost any form of work, creativity, learning, entertainment, relationships, or activity.

The question is:

WHERE WILL YOU DIRECT IT?

In that sense, we are the drivers of our own energy. We have a remarkable ability to choose what receives our attention, effort, and time.

And that gives us a degree of control over the direction of our lives.

MASTER YOUR ENERGY.
DIRECT YOUR THOUGHTS.
BUILD THE LIFE YOU WANT.

I tell you—you can become many things in this life. The world may place obstacles in your way, and other people may doubt you, but the key to what you do with your life is, to a significant extent, in your hands.

REDIRECT YOUR ENERGY TOWARD WHAT YOU WANT TO BECOME.

2. THE GREATEST FORCE TO COMPLETE ANY GREAT WORK

For us to complete anything truly great, we need PERSISTENCE.

But persistence does not always come naturally.

I believe persistence becomes much stronger when we are working toward something we genuinely desire.

That is why choosing something we truly care about—especially when it comes to a career, a purpose, or a major life goal—is so important.

When you strongly desire something, you are more willing to continue when things become difficult.

STRONG DESIRE CREATES STRONGER PERSISTENCE.

And when STRONG DESIRE and PERSISTENCE come together, they create an incredibly powerful force.

Obstacles become less frightening.

Failure becomes less final.

Setbacks become lessons rather than stopping points.

You keep moving.
You keep trying.
You keep building.
You keep going.

Life can make the journey difficult, but a person with an exceptionally strong desire and the persistence to keep pursuing it can become extraordinarily difficult to stop.

I believe this is one of the greatest forces behind the completion of any great work.

The daily energy within us, when consistently redirected toward something meaningful— especially one major goal—can produce a powerful desire.

That strong desire, combined with persistence, becomes a force capable of carrying a person through years of difficulty.

DESIRE + PERSISTENCE = THE FORCE TO FINISH.

If you want to accomplish something great, don't only ask yourself:

"How can I start?"

Ask yourself:

"What do I desire strongly enough to keep pursuing when it becomes difficult?"

Because starting is easy.

CONTINUING IS THE TEST.

And finishing is where the power of persistence reveals itself.

3. THE GREATEST POWER HUMANS WILL EVER KNOW — THE SUBCONSCIOUS MIND

One of the most fascinating discoveries I have made is the incredible power of the subconscious mind.

The human mind is capable of processing information, forming patterns, influencing behaviour, shaping habits, and directing our attention in ways we are not always consciously aware of.

Because of this, I believe the subconscious mind can play a powerful role in helping us move toward the things we genuinely desire.

One method I have found particularly interesting is the practice of writing down what you want.

When you clearly write down a goal or desire, you give your conscious and subconscious mind something specific to focus on.

And when you combine that written desire with faith, expectation, and action, something interesting can happen.

You begin noticing possibilities that you may have previously overlooked.

You start thinking differently.

You become more alert to opportunities.

You may meet people, discover information, notice resources, or recognize possibilities that can move you closer to what you want.

Sometimes the opportunity can appear so naturally that it feels almost like a miracle.

This is why I find the subconscious mind so fascinating.

It may not be that simply writing something down magically causes it to appear. Rather, writing it down can change what we pay attention to, what we look for, and how we respond to the world around us.

In that sense, the mind can become a powerful partner in achieving our goals.

MY METHOD

Here is the method I use:

STEP ONE — WRITE IT DOWN

Write down whatever you genuinely want or desire at that particular moment.

Be specific.

Don't simply think about it.

WRITE IT.

STEP TWO — KNOW THE PRICE

If the thing you want has a financial price, find out what that price is.

Write the price beside it.

If it has no financial price—such as a personal goal, experience, relationship, skill, or achievement—simply write the goal as clearly as possible.

New computer — K______

Learn a new skill

Complete my project

Travel to ______

Start a business

The purpose is to turn a vague desire into something concrete.

STEP THREE — HAVE FAITH AND EXPECT IT

Believe that it is possible.

Begin expecting it.

But don't just sit and wait.

PAY ATTENTION.

Look for opportunities.

Take action.

Talk to people.

Learn.

Save.

Work.

Prepare yourself.

Sometimes the opportunity may come from somewhere you never expected.

You may suddenly discover a way to obtain what you wanted.

Someone may introduce you to an opportunity.

You may find a resource you didn't know existed.

Or you may realize that you already had the ability to create the thing yourself.

And when that happens, it can feel almost like the universe opened a door.

BOOM.

The thing you wrote down is suddenly becoming real.

Perhaps sooner than you expected.

That is what fascinates me about the relationship between thought, belief, attention, action, and opportunity.

Sometimes we don't realize how much our minds influence the way we see and interact with the world.

So my discovery is this:

WHAT YOU THINK ABOUT MATTERS.
WHAT YOU WRITE DOWN MATTERS.
WHAT YOU EXPECT MATTERS.
WHAT YOU ACT UPON MATTERS.

The mind is powerful.

Desire is powerful.

Persistence is powerful.

And when all three work together, a person can accomplish things that once seemed impossible.

THE BIGGER IDEA

Perhaps the greatest lesson is not that we can simply write down anything and magically receive it.

The greater lesson is that a clearly defined desire can change the way we think, what we notice, what we pursue, and what we are willing to do.

WRITE THE VISION.
BELIEVE IT IS POSSIBLE.
PREPARE FOR IT.
WORK TOWARD IT.
WATCH FOR THE OPPORTUNITY.
AND WHEN THE OPPORTUNITY APPEARS—TAKE IT.

FINAL THOUGHT

There are forces within every human being that are easy to underestimate.

THOUGHT.
DESIRE.
ATTENTION.
PERSISTENCE.
ACTION.

We carry them every day.

The question is not whether we have energy.

WHERE ARE WE PUTTING IT?

Because whatever receives our energy, attention, and persistence has a much greater chance of becoming part of our lives.

So choose carefully.

REDIRECT YOUR ENERGY.
CONTROL YOUR THOUGHTS.
CHOOSE YOUR DESIRE.
BUILD YOUR PERSISTENCE.
TAKE ACTION.

And see what you can become.
